## Why not force-upgrade

`brace-expansion` 5.x exports named symbols (`{ expand }`) from its
CJS entry, while the 1.x consumers (`minimatch` 3.x inside the ESLint
family) do `const expand = require("brace-expansion")` and call it
directly. Pinning 5.0.8 tree-wide via `overrides` was tested and makes
the callable-module contract fail (`expand` becomes an object) —
ESLint's glob matching would crash. The exception is temporary until
upstream ESLint / minimatch move to a patched major, or the advisory
range is narrowed.
